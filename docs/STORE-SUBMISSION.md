# Priprema za AppStore i Google Play

## 1. Opis aplikacije

**Hrvatski:**
"Bela Games" je mobilna aplikacija za igru belota — tradicionalne kartaške igre bez ulaganja novca. Korisnici igraju s drugim igračima ili botima u javnom lobiju ili privatnim sobama s kodom. Aplikacija uključuje tablicu za ocjenjivanje ("blok"), fiksne emoji reakcije za komunikaciju, profil igrača, ocjenu pouzdanosti (karma) i nativne mogućnosti poput push obavijesti i haptic povratne informacije.

**English:**
"Bela Games" is a mobile app for playing belot — a traditional trick-taking card game with no money wagering. Users play against other players or bots in a public lobby or private code-protected rooms. The app includes a scorepad ("blok"), fixed emoji reactions for communication, player profiles, a reliability karma score, and native features such as push notifications and haptic feedback.

## 2. App Review Notes

**For Apple and Google reviewers:**

Bela Games is a free, multiplayer card game application based on belot, a traditional trick-taking card game. **No real money wagering occurs.** The app is purely recreational with no in-app purchases, no ads, and no tracking or analytics.

**Communication:** The app contains no text-based chat, user-generated content forums, or messaging. User-generated content is limited to a filtered display name (max 16 characters) and an avatar image; fixed emoji reactions only; no free-text communication. Room names are generated by the server. There is no report or block feature.

**Account management:** Users can delete their account from the in-app profile section. A public account deletion page is available at https://bela.games/brisanje-racuna.

**Sign-in:** The app supports email/password (Firebase), Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In, and guest play.

**Native features:** Push notifications (for game invitations and game starts), iOS Live Activity / Dynamic Island and Android 16 Live Updates (game in progress—visible when the phone is locked or the app is backgrounded; to see them: start a game against bots from the lobby and lock the phone or leave the app), haptic feedback, and deep linking for room invitations.

## 6. Česti razlozi odbijanja i zaštita

1. **Igre na sreću ili simulirana klađenja** — Aplikacija jasno nema novčanih uloga. Odmah je jasno u upitnicima.
2. **Nejasna privatnost** — Politika privatnosti je dostupna i opisuje samo potrebne podatke.
3. **Brisanje računa nije dostupno** — Dostupna je javna stranica i opcija u aplikaciji.
4. **Chat ili UGC rizik** — Nema chat-a, samo fiksne emoji reakcije, imena profila (filtrirana za nametljive nazive) i profilne slike. Nema mogućnosti prijave ili blokade korisnika.
5. **Niskokvalitetni screenshot-i ili opis** — Trebaju biti professionalni i detaljni, bez marketinškog praznog teksta.
